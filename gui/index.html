Methods marked sees recent actuals read observed demand from previous weeks at inference time — a real information advantage over the frozen models. Among models frozen at training time, the conditions model wins MAE and RMSE.

Relative WAPE improvement of the conditions model over history alone, block-bootstrapped over whole test days (10,000 replicates, seed 42). The day is the resampling unit because one storm correlates every error inside it. An interval clear of zero is a real improvement.
